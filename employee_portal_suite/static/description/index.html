Employee Portal Suite

A complete employee self-service, approvals, attendance, payroll reporting and internal communication workspace for Odoo 18.

Employee Self-ServiceApprovalsGPS AttendanceDiscuss & CallsWeb PushPWA

One Portal for Daily Employee Operations

Give portal employees a focused workspace without granting backend access. Employees can submit requests, follow approvals, check attendance, communicate through Odoo Discuss, receive push notifications and access authorized reports from one responsive portal.

Employee Requests

Submit employee requests with automatic numbering, manager/HR/Finance/CEO approval stages, comments, rejection handling, timelines and printable PDF reports.

Material Requests

Project-aware material requests with purchase, store, project manager, director and CEO stages; work locations, project assignment, quotations, accounting documents, PO creation and vendor bill support.

GPS Attendance & Geofencing

Portal check-in/check-out with work-location and project geofencing, attendance exceptions, automatic checkout support and controlled employee project locations.

Instant Salary & Attendance Report

Generate salary-oriented attendance reports filtered by company, department, employee tags and work location, including payroll values, deductions, reimbursements and printable reports.

Native Discuss in the Portal

Bring Odoo Discuss conversations and channels into the employee portal with portal-safe access, direct messaging, presence and an employee-friendly communication experience.

Audio & Video Calls

Integrated portal call experience with audio/video calling, call events and notifications tied to the employee communication workspace.

Web Push Notifications

Browser/PWA push notifications for approvals, stage changes, attendance events, reminders, escalations, chat messages and calls without requiring Telegram.

PWA Experience

Install the Employee Portal to the home screen for an app-like experience on supported desktop and mobile browsers.

Approvals Center

Managers and authorized users get dedicated approval views for employee and material requests with secure role-based access.

Documents, Announcements & Sign

Portal announcements, authorized report documents, purchase-order visibility and Odoo Sign document access are integrated into the employee experience.

Approval Workflows

Employee Request: Employee → Manager → HR → Finance → CEO
Material Request: Employee → Purchase → Store → Project Manager → Director → CEO

Designed for Controlled Portal Access

The suite uses dedicated security groups and record rules so portal employees work through a controlled self-service layer while managers and administrators retain the appropriate approval and backend controls.

Optional integrations: compatible bridge modules can automatically connect the Employee Portal Suite with other installed apps such as Petty Cash Management, Construction Contract Management and Treasury/Payment Planning when both sides are available.
